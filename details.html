what is active recall?

Active recall is as study method where you retrieve information from memory instead of re-reading notes.

it is also called the testing effectand is one of the most researched learning strategies.

The Science

Each time you recall something,the memory becomes stronger. students using active recall outperform others by up to 50%

How to pratice It

Method 1: flashcards with ANKI

ANKI uses a spaced repitition algorithm to show cards just before you forget them.

Method 2: blank page

Write down everythingyou remember about a topic without looking at notes, then check your gaps.

Method 3: Teach others

Explaining a concept out loud forces you to retrieve and organize the information — the feyman techniques.
